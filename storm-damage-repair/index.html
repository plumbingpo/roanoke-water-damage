A Roanoke property can take on a lot of water when a hurricane, a tropical storm, a flash flood or a heavy downpour hits. Gaps in siding, broken glass and roof damage all let wind-blown rain into the building. Once the roof leaks, water saturates wall cavities, ceilings and insulation. During a flash flood, drains cannot keep up and water ends up in the lowest floors. When a storm damages your property, Roanoke Restoration Co responds on an emergency basis to limit water intrusion, extract standing water and start restoration right away.

Our storm damage repair covers the whole job, from emergency board-up and tarping that keep more water out, through water extraction, structural drying and rebuilding what was damaged. We know a storm rarely leaves just one problem, because water entering through roof damage harms walls and ceilings and then feeds mold when drying is not done right. A single restoration plan from our team covers each of these related issues together.

After storm damage your insurer will want records, and we supply them: photos, moisture readings, written damage assessments and a detailed scope of work. How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor

What a Professional Brings to Storm Damage Repair

The repairs a storm leaves behind are tangled together, and sorting them out correctly calls for an experienced restoration crew. What seems like a small leak indoors can hide widespread water damage in wall cavities, roof decking and insulation, which turns into mold and structural decay unless it is identified and dried properly. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water paths and identify every affected area.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. An improperly installed tarp can create new problems by channeling water or failing under wind load. Our crews are experienced in storm damage mitigation and use the right materials and methods to give reliable temporary protection while permanent repairs are scheduled.

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Runoff should flow away from your foundation, so fix low spots and add downspout extensions
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe

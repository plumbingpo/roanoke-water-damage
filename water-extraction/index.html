Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Roanoke Restoration Co provides emergency water extraction throughout Roanoke using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Store a bucket and spare towels where you can grab them, along with the number for a restoration company
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Program (951) 612-5477 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • A clearly labeled main water valve, in a spot everyone knows, saves time in an emergency
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
